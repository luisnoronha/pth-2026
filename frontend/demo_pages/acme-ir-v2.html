Acme Corp reports third quarter 2026 results

Lisbon, 28 October 2026 · 07:00 WET

Acme Corp (ticker: ACME) today reported results for the third quarter ended 30 September 2026. Revenue grew 6% year on year to EUR 412 million, driven by industrial automation contracts in Iberia and a stronger services mix.

Highlights

  • Revenue of EUR 412M, up 6% year on year.
  • EBITDA of EUR 78M; operating margin of 14.2%.
  • Order book at a record EUR 1.3B.
  • Net debt reduced to 1.1x EBITDA.
  • Ana Ribeiro appointed Chief Financial Officer in September.
“A solid quarter. Our order book gives us good visibility into 2027, and we keep investing in the service business.”
Marta Leal, Chief Executive Officer
